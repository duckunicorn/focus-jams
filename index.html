Welcome to Focus Jams

A pomodoro timer made for a projector or TV, with music, and everyone's phones following along.

This guide sets up the parts that need accounts. Every step is optional: skip any of them and the timer still works. Doing everything takes about 15 minutes, once.

  1. Music: use the built-in Spotify player, or let the timer control your own Spotify app.
  2. Sync server: your own free Cloudflare server, so phones stay in sync with no limits.
  3. Shared archive: history and photos shared between all your screens.
  4. Your room: a name for this screen.
Used Focus Jams before on another computer? Load your settings backup (Customize → 💾 Backup → Download backup there) to bring everything over and skip the setup.

Works when the timer controls your Spotify app (My Spotify / Jam). Spotify's built-in player doesn't let websites change its volume, so it still stops and starts instantly. A few devices (some speakers and TVs) don't allow volume changes either.

With your Spotify app connected and playlist switching on, the timer starts the focus or break playlist on whatever device is playing. In a Jam, that changes the music for everyone listening.

Back up your setup

Save everything to one file: colors, background (including an uploaded picture), timer and sound settings, your custom sounds with their lengths, playlists, visualizer settings, group settings, Jam link, and room and people history. Load it on another computer or browser to get the same setup.

Not included: your Spotify sign-in (just click Connect on the new computer), group photos (download them from the night's recap) and the phone sync code (each computer gets its own QR code, so two screens never clash). Loading a backup replaces this browser's current setup.
